What's new – NovaTV 1.7.0

  • Telegram is now part of the build: the TeleScope AI engine (plugin.video.telescope 1.2.0) is installed automatically from this repository.
  • "Search all sources", every movie and every episode also show verified Telegram sources (exact season/episode, link and speed tested). Chiko Media channels you joined are ranked first.
  • Works with an installed Telemedia login, or with its own Telegram login. Optional Gemini AI for Hebrew / Russian title matching.
  • Wizard 0.2.1: an update no longer removes add-ons installed from the repository; failed downloads are cleaned up.
